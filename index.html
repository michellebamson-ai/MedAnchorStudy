Core capabilities

Everything students need to study, apply, research, and retain health-science knowledge.

MedAnchor is designed around the full learning lifecycle: understand content, apply it in realistic scenarios, evaluate evidence, prepare for assessments, and track improvement across the curriculum.

💬

Teach Me Mode

Interactive Socratic tutoring that guides learners toward understanding instead of giving away answers.

  • Adaptive explanation depth and difficulty
  • Step-by-step questioning and misconception feedback
  • Course material-aware tutoring and guided practice
📚

Analyze Docs

Students can transform PDFs, lecture slides, notes, audio, and images into structured learning resources.

  • Topic breakdowns and concept extraction
  • Revision notes, flashcards, and practice questions
  • Custom output formats and learning objectives
🩺

Case Simulators

Real-world clinical and public-health scenarios turn theory into practical decision-making and reasoning.

  • History-taking and differential diagnosis
  • Outbreaks, intervention planning, and policy thinking
  • Feedback loops tied to each learner’s profile
📊

Biostatistics & Research

Students can work through statistics, research design, interpretation, and methodology with adaptive guidance.

  • Hypothesis testing, OR/RR, sensitivity, specificity
  • Study design and variable selection
  • Formula explanations and result interpretation
🔎

Health Knowledge & Evidence

Research support helps students explore reliable health information, compare sources, and understand evidence.

  • Peer-reviewed literature and guidelines
  • Citations, source transparency, and limitations
  • Plain-language explanations for complex health topics
🗓️

Exam Prep & Smart Study Planning

Personalized academic planning reduces overload and keeps study time aligned with deadlines and weaknesses.

  • Study calendars, deadlines, and daily goals
  • Time-aware recommendations and catch-up logic
  • Progress forecasting with realistic planning
Who it is for

Built for healthcare students navigating dense content, high expectations, and long study cycles.

Medical students

Overloaded by content

Students often face dense lectures, long reading lists, and too much material to organize efficiently.

Hard to translate theory into practice

Many learners struggle to apply classroom concepts to patient scenarios, research questions, or public-health problems.

Weaknesses are not always visible

Students need adaptive feedback and connection between activity, performance, and future study actions.

Closed-loop learning

One study activity should improve the next.

MedAnchor is not a static dashboard. It creates a continuous cycle where each learning interaction informs the next review, practice session, and recommendation.

1

Upload

Lectures, PDFs, slides, notes, audio, and images become learning context.

2

Analyze

Topics, formulas, concepts, and weak points are extracted and organized.

3

Practice

Teach Me sessions and case simulations test understanding in realistic contexts.

4

Research

Evidence and statistics support deeper understanding and academic work.

5

Review

Spaced repetition and planner adjustments reinforce what needs attention next.

Research & Evidence Engine

Personalization & Customization

MedAnchor adapts to academic level, prior performance, courses, preferred explanation style, learning goals, and upcoming assessments without assuming everyone learns the same way.

🎧

Voice & Audio Learning

Audio and voice should support tutoring, revision, case simulations, clinical communication practice, and transcript-based study workflows.

🔗

Connectors & Integrations

Google Calendar, Google Drive, OneDrive, LMS systems like Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, and Notion integrations help bring academic tasks into context.

⏳

Spaced Repetition

Reviews are scheduled at intervals based on mastery, forgetting, and upcoming exams so weak material is reinforced at the right time.

📈

Connected Progress Tracking

Strengths, weaknesses, study consistency, and topic mastery are tracked across tutoring, cases, research, and planning into actionable recommendations.

Trust and usability

Designed to be clear, secure, accessible, and academically responsible.

Accessibility & usability

Mobile-first, responsive, low-cognitive-load design with dark mode, readable hierarchy, captions, transcripts, and support for assistive technologies across long study sessions.

Privacy & security

Uploaded materials, conversations, and personal data are treated as sensitive information with secure storage, transmission, access controls, and clear student-managed deletion and integration permissions.

Accuracy & academic integrity

MedAnchor prioritizes evidence-based explanations, source transparency, uncertainty communication, and support for original student work rather than copy-and-submit shortcuts.
